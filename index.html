The Artist

I believe light is the most powerful tool to tell a story without words.

A film student who is deeply committed to the camera department as a form of visual storytelling. Through hand-on work on student productions and indie productions, I have deveoped a practice centred on natural light, real locations, and precise focus techniques to convey emotion and narrative without relying on dialogue. I believe the most honest cinema reveals truth through the frame—not through stylisation, but through careful observation and techical discipline.
